What this number means
  • Razorpay test mode
    Recovery recorded from test gateway responses or verified webhooks. No production money moves.
  • Simulated batch
    Outcomes depend on the customer model. This measures engine behavior under stated assumptions, not proven commercial uplift.
  • CSV preview
    A classification and schedule review of imported obligations. No payment success is invented.
Controls built into the engine
  • 22:00–08:00 IST quiet hours and per-customer caps
  • Stable operation identities; uncertain writes held for reconciliation
  • Fresh payment-state checks before live actions
  • Opt-outs and promises checked before customer contact
  • AI suggestions pass deterministic validation
  • Disputes and unreadable replies go to a human
  • Signed webhooks and customer opt-out links
Account capabilities
Saved-instrument charging is unavailable in the current Razorpay adapter. Payment-link method exclusions are recorded as intent; enforcement is not claimed. The mandate path is exercised by the simulated gateway.

SMS & email delivery
Razorpay sends its standard payment-link notification to the known contact on a case. No additional email provider is required. Email-only cases use email; phone-only cases use SMS. Unknown contacts are never messaged.
These choices apply to the next “Create test links” action in Batch lab. Every sending action asks for confirmation. Test mode prevents real payment collection, but notifications may reach real people. Use recipients who agreed to testing. Razorpay sandbox limits may prevent sending; delivery is not guaranteed.
